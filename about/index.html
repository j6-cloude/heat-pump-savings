About Heat Pump Savings Check

By j6 · Updated October 27, 2026 · Prices from EIA (dates on each page) · Estimates, not quotes

Heat Pump Savings Check is a free, independent calculator that helps people see if a heat pump would lower their heating bill. It uses public data from the EIA, NOAA, the IRS and the U.S. Department of Energy.
