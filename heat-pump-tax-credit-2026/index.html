Is there still a heat pump tax credit?

By j6 · Updated October 28, 2026 · Prices from EIA (dates on each page) · Estimates, not quotes

Yes, it ended. The 2025 tax law (Public Law 119-21) ended the 25C Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit for property placed in service after December 31, 2025 (IRS).

What it was

25C gave 30% of the cost of a qualifying heat pump, up to $2,000 a year. It applied to heat pumps placed in service from 2023 through 2025.

Who can still claim it

If your heat pump was placed in service in 2025, you claim it on your 2025 tax return using IRS Form 5695. A system installed in 2026 doesn't qualify.

What's left

State and utility rebates, and federal HEAR/HOMES rebates in states that run them. See the 2026 rebate guide.

Questions people ask

Can I claim 25C for a heat pump installed in January 2026?

No. The IRS says no credit is allowed for property placed in service after December 31, 2025.

I paid in 2025 but it was installed in 2026. Do I qualify?

25C depends on when the property is placed in service. Check the IRS FAQ and a tax professional.
