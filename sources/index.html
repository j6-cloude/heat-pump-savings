Sources and data

By j6 · Updated October 28, 2026 · Prices from EIA (dates on each page) · Estimates, not quotes

All prices come from free public downloads from the U.S. Energy Information Administration. No API keys or paid data. We re-check when EIA publishes new data.

Prices: electricity = Residential average, January–August 2026 (August 2026 YTD) (EIA Table 5.6.B). Natural gas = Residential average of monthly prices, November 2025–March 2026 (last heating season) (EIA). Heating oil and propane = EIA weekly residential price, week of 2026-10-19; states EIA doesn't survey use the regional or U.S. price. Climate = NOAA heating degree days. You can type your own prices.

  • EIA Electric Power Monthly, Table 5.6.B: residential electricity price by state, year to date
  • EIA: Natural gas prices by state (residential, monthly)
  • EIA Heating Oil and Propane Update (weekly residential prices)
  • EIA Winter Fuels Outlook, October 2026
  • EIA: British thermal units (Btu) and fuel heat content
  • NOAA NCEI Climate at a Glance / nClimDiv statewide degree days
  • NOAA U.S. Climate Normals 1991–2020
  • EIA Residential Energy Consumption Survey (RECS) 2020: main space heating fuel by state
  • IRS: FAQs on changes to 25C, 25D and other credits under the One Big Beautiful Bill (P.L. 119-21)
  • IRS: Energy Efficient Home Improvement Credit (25C)
  • U.S. Department of Energy: Home Energy Rebates program
  • DOE Home Energy Rebates Program Notice 26-2 (HEAR/HEEHR changes)
  • NASEO: Home Energy Rebates FAQ, September 2026
  • ENERGY STAR: State and Tribal rebate programs (HEAR)
  • ENERGY STAR: Air-source heat pumps
  • ENERGY STAR: Ductless heating and cooling (mini-splits)
  • NEEP: Cold-climate air-source heat pump product list
  • AHRI Directory of certified product performance

Hawaii, Alaska and DC climate use NOAA 1991–2020 airport station normals (Honolulu, Anchorage, Washington Reagan) because the statewide series doesn't cover them. All other states use NOAA nClimDiv statewide heating degree days, 1991–2020 average.
