HSPF2 to COP converter

By j6 · Updated October 27, 2026 · Prices from EIA (dates on each page) · Estimates, not quotes

Average seasonal COP

The math: HSPF2 is Btu of heat per watt-hour of electricity over a test season. One watt-hour = 3.412 Btu. So COP ≈ HSPF2 ÷ 3.412. An HSPF2 of 8.5 ≈ COP 2.49.

The HSPF2 test season represents a moderate climate. In colder states, real COP is lower. Our calculator lowers it by 10% for states with 5,000–7,000 heating degree days and 20% above 7,000. This is our planning assumption, not a test result.
