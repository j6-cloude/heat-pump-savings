Things that change the answer

  • Replacing an air conditioner too? A heat pump also cools, so compare against the cost of a new AC plus a new furnace, not zero.
  • Rebates: utility rebates can be large. Get them in writing.
  • Fuel prices: oil prices change a lot from year to year (EIA).
  • Insulation: sealing air leaks first lowers the size and cost of the system.

Questions people ask

How much does a heat pump cost?

It varies by home size, ducts, brand and region. Get at least three written quotes. We don't publish price averages we can't verify.

What is a good payback time?

Shorter is better. Many people also count comfort, summer cooling and replacing old equipment, not just the payback years.
