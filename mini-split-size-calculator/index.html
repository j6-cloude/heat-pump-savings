Mini-split size calculator

By j6 · Updated October 28, 2026 · Prices from EIA (dates on each page) · Estimates, not quotes

This gives a rough size to help you compare quotes. A good installer should do a room-by-room load calculation (called Manual J) before you buy.

Heat needed on the coldest days

How we estimate it

We use the same heat-loss assumption as our savings calculator (3, 4 or 5.5 Btu per sq ft per degree day for good, average or drafty homes). Heat loss per hour = that number × area ÷ 24 × (65°F − outdoor temperature). It's a planning number, not a design.

Why the cold-temperature number matters

Heat pumps make less heat when it's colder. Check how much heat a model makes at 5°F on the NEEP cold-climate list or its spec sheet, not just its name ("12k", "18k").

Questions people ask

How many square feet does a 12,000 Btu mini split heat?

It depends on insulation and climate. In our average-insulation example at 5°F outdoors, 12,000 Btu/hr covers about 1,200 sq ft. Drafty homes need more.

Is a bigger heat pump better?

No. Too big can short-cycle and feel less comfortable. Size it to your home's heat loss.
