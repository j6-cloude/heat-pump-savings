Portable and window heat pumps

By j6 · Updated October 27, 2026 · Prices from EIA (dates on each page) · Estimates, not quotes

A space heater turns 1 kWh of power into 3,412 Btu of heat (COP 1). A small window or portable heat pump can make 2 or more times that much heat from the same power, when it isn't too cold outside.

Things to know

  • Portable units with one hose can pull cold air into the room, which lowers real savings.
  • Window heat pumps are popular for apartments where you can't install a mini-split.
  • Look for the heating capacity at low temperatures on the spec sheet.
  • During a power outage, no electric heater works without backup power. See Ready.gov.

Questions people ask

Is a window heat pump cheaper than a space heater?

Yes, when it's working as a heat pump. At COP 2 it uses about half the electricity for the same heat.
